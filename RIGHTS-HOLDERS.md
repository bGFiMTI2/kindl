# Rights-holder notice process

The maintainers intend to respect valid intellectual-property rights and to
resolve substantiated concerns in good faith. This process covers material in
this repository; the maintainers cannot control third-party forks, user data,
or conduct outside the project.

## Sending a notice

Please contact the repository owner privately using the contact method on the
owner's GitHub profile. If no private contact method is available, use GitHub's
applicable copyright, trademark, or abuse-reporting channel rather than posting
confidential information in a public issue.

A notice should include:

- the claimant's name and contact information;
- confirmation that the sender is the rights holder or is authorized to act;
- the right, work, mark, confidential material, or other interest concerned;
- exact repository URLs and enough detail to identify the challenged material;
- the jurisdiction and a concise explanation of the asserted legal basis;
- the requested corrective action; and
- a good-faith statement that the supplied information is accurate.

Do not include Amazon credentials, private keys, tokens, signed URLs, vouchers,
downloaded books, or unnecessary personal data. A cryptographic hash or a
precise textual description is preferable when it can identify the material.

## Review and response

The maintainers will acknowledge and review a sufficiently complete notice as
promptly as reasonably possible. They may request evidence of authority or
clarification, preserve relevant records, temporarily restrict disputed
material while investigating, and correct or remove material where warranted.
Where appropriate and legally permitted, the contributor responsible for the
material may be offered an opportunity to respond.

Knowingly false, misleading, or abusive notices may cause harm. Please consider
applicable exceptions, limitations, interoperability rights, fair use or fair
dealing, and other lawful uses before submitting a demand.

This voluntary process is not an admission of infringement or unlawful
conduct, does not designate a statutory service agent, and does not waive any
defense, exception, jurisdictional objection, or other right. A court order or
valid legal obligation will be handled in accordance with applicable law.
